Valhalla Browser Tools

Small browser-only tools for the power user.

Playgrounds

  • Route debugger /route Click waypoints on a map, tune location and costing options, and inspect the route's weights and turn weights or the attributes of its edges.
  • Locate visualizer /locate Click a map to send locate requests and see the edges and nodes each location snaps to.

Miscellaneous

  • Attributes controller builder Pick Valhalla attributes from a list and get the include or exclude filter as JSON.
